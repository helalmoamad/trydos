# Story Test Scenarios (Short Checklist)

- Upload an image story and verify it is successfully uploaded and visible in the user's story list.
- Upload a video story and verify it is successfully uploaded and visible in the user's story list.
- Verify a new story is added to the existing story group for that user when the group already exists.
- Verify a new story group is created automatically when the user has no existing story group.
- Verify story pagination works correctly when total stories are more than 10 (load next items without duplicates or missing stories).
- Verify that when opening a story, the timeline/progress bar does not start until the story image is fully loaded.
